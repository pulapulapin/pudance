PuDance Support

How to use PuDance, and how to report a bug.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 15 or later (TestFlight / App Store).
  • Mac build is available for development; Mac App Store listing comes later.
  • A dance video on your device that you have the right to practice with.

Getting started

  1. Install PuDance from TestFlight or the App Store when available.
  2. Allow Photos / camera access when you import a clip or use follow-along.
  3. Create a Pack from your reference video.
  4. Use slow, mirror, and loop controls while you practice.
  5. Optional: open follow-along to record a take next to the reference—saved on device only.

What PuDance can and can’t do

CanCan’t
Import local / Photos videos for practiceDownload dances from the web or strip watermarks
Slow, mirror, A–B loop, follow-along cameraUpload your clips to a social feed

Process video on deviceBypass DRM or access private streams

Questions

Does PuDance upload my videos?

No. Practice clips and camera takes stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No.

Where do my Packs live?

In the app’s local storage on that device.

How do I get a refund for an App Store purchase?

Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I practice copyrighted choreography?

Only import videos you have the right to use for personal practice. PuDance does not provide download or sharing of third-party content.

Report a bug

This GitHub repo is the support channel. Please include:

  • What you did, what you expected, what happened.
  • Device model, iOS / macOS version, and PuDance version (Settings).
  • Whether it was import, playback, mirror/slow/loop, or follow-along.
