Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

In one sentence

PuDance works on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting to us. We do not collect, receive or upload your practice videos. On the App Store, the privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Where your data lives

DataWhereNotes
Imported practice videos / PacksApp sandbox on your deviceStays on that device
Follow-along recordings (if you use them)App sandbox or Photos if you save thereStays on that device
Settings (mute default, UI preferences)App preferences on deviceStays on that device

When PuDance uses the internet

Only because of something you or the system does:

  • Installing or updating the app from the App Store / TestFlight (handled by Apple).
  • If you buy or restore an in-app purchase: payment is handled by Apple; PuDance only learns whether the purchase is valid.
  • Opening this website or filing a GitHub issue (standard web / GitHub traffic).

PuDance does not send your dance videos to our servers. We do not run a user-content sync server.

Camera, microphone and Photos

Camera and microphone are used only for optional follow-along preview / recording. Photos / Files access is used only so you can import videos you choose. Permission dialogs explain this in the app. You can deny access; import or follow-along features that need that permission will not work.

Purchases

App Store purchases, receipts and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Delete Packs and recordings inside the app, or delete the app to remove its sandbox data.
  • Revoke camera / Photos access in iOS or macOS Settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions or bug reports: GitHub Issues / 839200644@qq.com.
